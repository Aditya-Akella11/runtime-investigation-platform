# 01_PRODUCT_VISION

## Product Vision
The Runtime Investigation Platform exists to help engineering teams move from incident symptoms to root cause with speed, consistency, and evidence.

## Mission
To give teams a unified workflow for investigating runtime issues, collecting evidence, and coordinating remediation across applications, providers, and operational systems.

## Problem Statement
Modern applications often fail across distributed runtime environments with fragmented signals. Teams struggle to correlate customer impact, runtime behavior, provider telemetry, and manual investigation notes into a single actionable investigation.

## Why Existing Solutions Are Not Enough
Existing tools are often fragmented, provider-specific, or focused on monitoring rather than investigation workflows. They rarely support a complete lifecycle from issue intake to evidence capture and root cause confirmation.

## Our Differentiation
This platform combines investigation workflows, runtime probes, provider integration, and auditability into one structured system that supports both human investigation and future runtime agent automation.

## Customer Personas
- Engineering teams handling production incidents
- Site reliability engineers
- Platform and observability engineers
- Support and operations teams

## Core Product Principles
- Evidence over assumptions
- Structured investigation over ad hoc notes
- Provider-neutral integration
- Traceable workflows and auditability
- Incremental automation with human oversight

## MVP Goals
- Capture and manage investigations
- Support runtime probes and evidence collection
- Integrate with major runtime providers
- Provide a consistent user experience across investigation stages

## Future Vision
Expand into autonomous investigation workflows, deeper runtime agent orchestration, and richer cross-provider correlation.
